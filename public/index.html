StreetLens
Read with care. Police anonymise crime locations to a nearby point, so crimes appear on streets or landmarks near where they happened. Rates per 1,000 use resident population, so town centres and busy areas look higher. Police data is published about two months after each month ends and only includes crimes recorded with a location. Distances are straight-line. How to read this site. Open data and district feeds sit on the overview. Official community messages from the force are on eCops.
