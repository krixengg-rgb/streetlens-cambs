Read with care. Locations are anonymised by police to a nearby point, so crimes appear on streets or landmarks near where they happened. Rates per 1,000 use resident population, which makes town centres and busy areas (with many visitors) look higher. Data is published around two months after the month ends and only covers crimes recorded with a location.

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Crime data: data.police.uk (Cambridgeshire Constabulary). Population: ONS mid-year estimates via Nomis. Postcodes: postcodes.io (ONS/OS data). Map © OpenStreetMap contributors. StreetLens is an independent prototype, not affiliated with any police force.
